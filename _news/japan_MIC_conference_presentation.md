I presented a paper on the subject of solving the PET image reconstruction problem with diffusion models at the IEEE Nuclear Science Symposium Medical Imaging Conference (IEEE MIC).

My [work](/publications/#webber2025steerable_conditional_diffusion), presented orally, showed the benefits of inference-time adapation for using diffusion models to reconstruction PET image data. I also spoke about my recent advances in developing an overfitting-proof loss for PET image reconstruction, see [more details](/presentations/nov-2025-mic).
